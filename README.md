# Assignment Reader 🎧

**Your assignments are long. Your patience is short. Let the app do the reading.**

Assignment Reader is a text-to-speech desktop app built specifically for students who have a mountain of assignments to write and absolutely no desire to read through every source document manually.

Feed it your study material, hit play, put on your headphones, and let it read while you focus on writing. Think of it as having a personal narrator who doesn't get tired, ask for attendance, or complain about your handwriting.

### Why?
Because writing 20 pages of assignments is already painful. Reading 50 pages of reference material before that is just unnecessary character development.

### The idea
- Turn study material into spoken audio.
- Listen while writing your assignments.
- Spend less time reading and more time getting those pages filled.
- Keep the workflow simple and student-friendly.

**Built for students. Powered by text-to-speech. Fueled by assignment deadlines.**

*It won't write your assignments for you. But at least it can do the talking.*
